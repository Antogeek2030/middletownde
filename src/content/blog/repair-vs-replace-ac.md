When a major repair quote arrives, homeowners often ask: is it worth fixing, or is it time for a new system?

## Rules of Thumb

- **Age under 10 years** and a single isolated failure (capacitor, contactor, fan motor): repair usually makes sense.
- **Age 12+ years** with rising repair frequency or a compressor/coil failure: evaluate replacement.
- **Repair cost over 50% of a new system**: lean toward replacement, especially if efficiency is low.

## Efficiency and Comfort

Newer systems often use less electricity and handle humidity better. Proper sizing (Manual J) matters more than simply matching the old tonnage.
