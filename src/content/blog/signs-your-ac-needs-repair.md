Delaware summers are tough on air conditioners. Catching problems early can save you from a no-cool emergency and higher repair bills.

## 1. Weak or Warm Airflow

If vents blow lukewarm air or airflow feels weak, the system may have low refrigerant, a dirty filter, or a failing blower motor. Do not keep running a system that cannot cool properly.

## 2. Unusual Noises

Grinding, squealing, or banging usually means a mechanical part is wearing out. Continuing to run the unit can turn a motor repair into a full compressor replacement.

## 3. Water Leaks Indoors

Standing water near the indoor unit often points to a clogged condensate drain. In humid New Castle County weather, this can lead to floor damage and mold if ignored.

## 4. Short Cycling

An AC that turns on and off every few minutes wastes energy and stresses components. Causes include oversized equipment, thermostat issues, or refrigerant problems.

## 5. Rising Energy Bills

A sudden jump in electric costs without a change in usage often means the system is working harder due to dirty coils, low charge, or aging parts.

## What to Do Next

If you notice any of these signs in Middletown or nearby towns, schedule a diagnostic. Same-day service is often available, and a written estimate is provided before any repair work begins.
